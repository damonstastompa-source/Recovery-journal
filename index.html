💾 Data backup & transfer

Your journal is stored locally on this device/browser. Use a backup file before updating the app or moving your journal to another installation.

Importing replaces the data currently stored in this app with the backup you choose.

How to update or transfer your save data
  1. Before updating: open the old app that contains your journal and go to Settings → Data backup → Export backup.
  2. Save or share the JSON backup somewhere you can find it again, such as Files or Messages to yourself.
  3. Install/open the updated Recovery Journal version.
  4. Go to Settings → Data backup → Import backup, then choose your saved JSON file.
  5. Confirm the import. Your journal entries, meetings, templates, topics, and clean date will be restored.
Important: Do not delete the old app or clear its website data until you have successfully exported and verified your backup.
