Recovery Journal v50.6.4.2

What is Recovery Journal?

A private, device-based journal built to help you pause each day, reflect on your recovery, keep track of meetings, and notice patterns in your thoughts and emotions.

Your Daily Entry

  • Affirmations — three positive statements for today.
  • Gratitude — three things you're grateful for.
  • Today's Focus — one thing you want to practice, remember, or carry with you today.
  • Living the Program — an optional daily reflection with an original prompt. Tap Another for a different reflection.
  • My Emotions — a dedicated screen for browsing, searching, filtering, editing, and reviewing your emotion history.
  • Emotion Log — slow down and record what you're feeling, how intense it was, what happened, how you responded, and what you need now.
  • Emotion Wheel — use the wheel when you know something is going on but need help finding the words.
  • Journal — write whatever you need to get out.
  • Meetings — record meetings, topics, notes, time, and place. If enabled in Settings, you can collect drawn chairperson initials for attendance records.

Daily Check-In

A quick daily snapshot of how you are doing. Choose a mood, then optionally expand Want to reflect a little more? to record what you need and one recovery action. Your check-in becomes part of that day’s journal.

Journal View

Open My Journal from the navigation menu to browse your recovery story chronologically. Search your entries, filter by date or category, and open any day in the Journal Reader. The Journal Reader includes Daily Check-In information when it was recorded, with subtle Today navigation for older days.

What Helped Me Before?

Use ☰ → What Helped Me Before? to search your own recovery history. Find past journal entries, gratitudes, affirmations, meetings, emotions, goals, and Messages to Myself by keyword or category.

My Goals

Track short-term and long-term goals, optional milestones, status, target dates, and personal reflections. Completed goals remain available from the Completed goals view.

Messages to Myself

Keep one or more personal letters for your future self. The letter icon at the top opens a clean, scrollable reading view. Create, edit, add, or delete messages from Settings → My Recovery → Messages to Myself. The optional Writing Guide is there if it helps.

Your Recovery

Your clean date powers your personal clean-time counter. You can change it from My Recovery → Clean Time.

Appearance

Choose from the existing themes plus Midnight Forest and Dark Burgundy. Themes are designed to keep text, controls, cards, and navigation readable throughout the app.

Your Recovery Community

Manage friends from the dedicated Friends page. Milestone reminder types are managed in Settings.

Privacy

Your journal and recovery information are stored locally on your device. Recovery Journal does not require an account or a server database for your journal data.

ℹ️ About Recovery Journal

Recovery Journal v50.6.4.2

What is Recovery Journal?

A private, device-based journal built to help you pause each day, reflect on your recovery, keep track of meetings, notice emotional patterns, and look back on your own progress.

Daily Check-In

A quick snapshot of how you are doing today. Choose a mood, then optionally expand Want to reflect a little more? to record what you need and one recovery action. Your check-in becomes part of that day's journal and appears in the Journal Reader.

My Journal & Journal Reader

Browse your recovery story by date, search and filter your entries, and open any saved day in the Journal Reader. The reader includes your Daily Check-In, affirmations, gratitude, focus, meetings, emotions, and journal writing when present. Use the subtle Today control or swipe between saved days.

My Emotions

Open ☰ → My Emotions to browse, search, filter, edit, and review your saved Emotion Logs. The Emotion Log remains available from Today as part of your daily reflection.

What Helped Me Before?

Search your own recovery history for past journal entries, gratitudes, affirmations, meetings, emotions, goals, and Messages to Myself. Results are drawn from your local journal data.

My Goals

Track short-term and long-term goals, target dates, progress, milestones, and completed goals from the navigation menu.

Messages to Myself

Write personal messages for your future self and read them from the message button at the top of the app. Messages can be added, edited, and managed from Settings.

Meetings & Reports

Record meeting time, place, name, topic, and notes. Optional chairperson initials can be collected for attendance records. Print attendance sheets and journal reports, including emotion information when appropriate.

Appearance

Choose from 11 themes: Serene, Dark, Moonlight, Neon, Ocean, Sunrise, Black Light, Cosmic, High Contrast, Midnight Forest, and Dark Burgundy. Theme choices stay on this device.

Privacy & Data

Your journal is designed to stay on your device. No account is required and journal data is not uploaded to a Recovery Journal server. Use Backup & Transfer before moving to another device or installation.

Note:

Recovery Journal is a tool for reflection. Use it in whatever way supports your recovery.

📱 How to install Recovery Journal

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Recovery Journal in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open Recovery Journal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation.
Keep your data safe

Recovery Journal stores your entries locally on the device/browser. If you are moving to another phone or installation, use Settings → Backup & Transfer → Export backup first, then import that backup on the new installation.

💾 Backup & Transfer

Your journal is stored locally on this device/browser. Use a backup file before updating the app or moving your journal to another installation.

Importing replaces the data currently stored in this app with the backup you choose.

How to update or transfer your save data
  1. Before updating: open the old app that contains your journal and go to Settings → Backup & Transfer → Export backup.
  2. Save or share the JSON backup somewhere you can find it again, such as Files or Messages to yourself.
  3. Install/open the updated Recovery Journal version.
  4. Go to Settings → Backup & Transfer → Import backup, then choose your saved JSON file.
  5. Confirm the import. Your journal entries, meetings, templates, topics, name, and clean date will be restored.
Important: Do not delete the old app or clear its website data until you have successfully exported and verified your backup.
